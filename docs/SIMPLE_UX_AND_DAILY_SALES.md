# Approved update: simple interface and daily sales

This update forms part of the app plan and supersedes earlier navigation proposals. It describes planned features, not implemented application code.

## Simple interface

- Four main sections: Home, Stock, Accounts, More. Accounts has clear tabs: We owe / Customers owe us.
- Home has three prominent actions: Add stock, Remove stock, Record payment. Low stock and unpaid balances appear below. Record sale is available within Remove stock and as a configurable Home shortcut.
- Essential fields first; invoice photos, references and notes under More details.
- Plain labels, large readable text, generous spacing, touch-sized controls and restrained gold accents.
- Recent products and remembered supplier choices speed up entry. Review remembered choices before saving.
- Profit stays on its own screen, accessible through More and optional shortcuts.
- A short payment offers Give discount or Keep remaining amount as credit. Customer selection is required for credit.
- Save unfinished entries, clearly show successful saves, and allow corrections with full change history.
- Sync quietly with a small status indicator; clearly flag pending changes and errors.
- Validate a clickable phone prototype with the owner and brother before full implementation.

## Daily sales recording is included

- Record an individual sale: items, quantities, selling amounts, whole-sale discount, payment method, amount received and any customer credit. Customer details are optional for fully paid sales.
- Alternatively record an end-of-day summary for sales not already entered individually. Enter sold quantities per product and corresponding sales amounts to update stock and calculate gross profit.
- Never record the same sale both individually and in the daily summary. Show individually recorded totals when preparing the summary and clearly label it as additional, unrecorded sales.
- A daily sales view shows sales before discounts, discounts, net sales, amounts paid at sale, credit created and gross profit on records with complete costs. Repayments of old customer debt are shown separately, not counted as new sales.
- Quantity-only stock-outs still work but cannot supply a sales amount or reliable actual profit. A single money-only daily total cannot automatically identify which products left stock; flag missing item detail rather than inventing it.
- Daily sales are available without making a receipt mandatory. Full POS, parked carts and cash-register closing remain later features.
- Corrections update stock, customer balances and sales/profit together, retaining history. Avoid deducting stock again when adding money details to an existing stock-out.
